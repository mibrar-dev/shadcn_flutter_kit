## Design decisions

1. **One shell on the wall.** Spec §2.1 pins home cards at 24 px radius;
   `StudioCard` resolves the themed `borderRadiusXl` (~14 px). Embedding
   `Studio*` widgets directly would mix two radii on one wall, so the
   studio blocks are *extended*, not embedded: same compositions, same
   sample data, same interior helpers (`StudioCaption/Value/Row/
   FieldLabel/Helper/Stat`, `StudioSparkline`, `StudioContributionChart`,
   `StudioQrTile` imported, not copied), re-shelled in `CollageCard`.
2. **Opened overlays, two shapes.** Trigger cards open the real routes
   (dialog / alert / drawer / sheet / command palette — all tapped open
   and closed in tests); the dropdown surface and menubar show the opened
   look inline. Matches the reference (triggers + open menu surfaces).
3. **Inline menus must not steal focus.** `MenuGroup` autofocuses its
   `RovingGroup` scope on mount (`menu_nav.dart:232`, default true). Two
   inline menu surfaces pulled initial focus into the wall, which broke
   `focus_test` (first Tab) and the `Ctrl+K` palette (probe: primary focus
   was `RovingGroup`, palette stayed shut). Both cards wrap their menus
   in `FocusScope(canRequestFocus: false)`; probe after the fix: initial
   focus is `header-focus-anchor`, `Ctrl+K` opens the palette. Mouse
   interaction is unaffected.
4. **No infinite-animation widgets on the wall.** `Skeleton` (repeating
   shimmer) and `Spinner` (repeating builder) keep the frame schedule
   alive, so `pumpAndSettle` — used by every landing test — never
   settles. Loading feedback is composed from static `Progress` bars
   (Storage card); skeleton/spinner stay covered on component pages.
5. **No `Stepper` on the wall.** It animates through an
   `AnimatedSwitcher`, and `motion_test` requires exactly one of those on
   the landing page (the mobile popper's). The checklist story uses
   `Checkbox` rows instead; the stepper is covered on its component page.
6. **Label collisions with site-wide tests.** The wall shares the page
   with the header, so card copy must not duplicate header/palette
   strings asserted with `findsOneWidget` elsewhere: breadcrumb `Home` →
   `Library`, command trigger `Search…` → `Commands…` (both verified free
   in `docs/test`).
7. **Min-width components scroll.** `Pagination` (intrinsic window) and
   `Calendar` (~280 px grid) keep the P6-T1 horizontal-scroll guard;
   `Menubar` gets the same guard (overflowed 16 px at 1400 in tests). A
   concurrent agent is shrinking `Calendar`/`Pagination` in the registry
   (uncommitted); the used APIs are unchanged there (verified by grep) and
   the scroll guards are harmless under both versions.
